❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many people have been killed in Gaza?
As of 2026-10-08, the Gaza Ministry of Health reports 74,254+ people killed, including 20,179+ children and 12,500+ women, with 175,306+ more injured.
Where does this data come from?
From the Gaza Ministry of Health's daily reports, aggregated and published in machine-readable form by Tech for Palestine, cross-referenced with UN OCHA figures.
Are these numbers accurate?
They are the official verified figures, and are considered minimum counts: victims still under the rubble and unidentified bodies are not fully included. The known-by-name victim list currently stands at 72,835+.
How often is this page updated?
Automatically, every day, by an open-source pipeline. The raw data is also available as JSON: latest statistics and full daily history.
